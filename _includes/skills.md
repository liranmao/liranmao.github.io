<h2 id="skills">Skills</h2>

- **[cv-to-homepage](https://github.com/liranmao/cv-to-homepage)** <br> Turns a CV into a personal website with customizable styles and backgrounds, and deploys it to GitHub Pages.
- **[daily-papers](https://github.com/liranmao/daily-papers)** <br> Creates personalized daily briefings from arXiv, PubMed, and AI news, with research summaries in text, audio, and video.
- **[paper-to-library](https://github.com/liranmao/paper-to-library)** <br> Discovers papers through email briefings and saves selected references to Zotero with linked English reading notes in Obsidian.
- **[awesome-CNS-skills](https://github.com/liranmao/awesome-CNS-skills)** <br> Provides reusable research skills for publication-quality figures, concise academic slides, and QuPath-compatible pathology annotations.
