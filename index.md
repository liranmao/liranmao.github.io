<h2 id="about-me">About Me</h2>
I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Graduate Group in Genomics and Computational Biology](https://www.med.upenn.edu/gcb/) at the Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania. I am fortunate to be co-advised by Prof. [Mingyao Li](https://transgen.med.upenn.edu/people/mingyao-li/) and Prof. [Yanxiang Deng](https://yanxiangdenglab.org/). I am affiliated with the [Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine](https://pathology.med.upenn.edu/), the [Statistical Center for Single-Cell and Spatial Genomics](https://dbei.med.upenn.edu/center-of-excellence/scscsg/) within the [Department of Biostatistics, Epidemiology and Informatics](https://dbei.med.upenn.edu/), and the [Department of Statistics and Data Science at The Wharton School](https://statistics.wharton.upenn.edu/).

My research focuses on digital pathology and spatial multi-omics. I develop multimodal deep learning methods for complex biomedical data, with expertise spanning foundation models, computational pathology, single-cell and spatial multi-omics, regulatory genomics, domain adaptation, and clinical outcome prediction.

Before my Ph.D., I worked in [Dr. Jason Buenrostro’s lab](https://www.buenrostrolab.com/) at Harvard University, studying stem cell aging through multi-omics analysis and deep learning, and in [Dr. Jian Zhou’s lab](https://www.zhoulab.io/) at the University of Chicago, working on sequence-to-function models.

I am actively seeking a summer 2027 internship.
